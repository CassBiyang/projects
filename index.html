Problem statement
Growmotely's customers — mid-market hiring companies and global enterprise recruitment teams — had no structured self-serve path for onboarding or ongoing platform guidance. All onboarding relied on 1:1 CSM walkthroughs (3–4 hours per customer), support tickets clustered around repetitive procedural questions, and internal knowledge was scattered across product docs, engineering specs, and Slack threads. There was no role-aware, searchable single source of truth.
Objectives
  • Deflect tier-1 support volume by enabling customer self-service for procedural questions
  • Reduce CSM onboarding time per new customer from 3–4 hours to under 1 hour
  • Establish a single, maintained source of truth for platform behavior, integrations, and workflows
  • Build role-aware content paths for five distinct user personas across the platform
  • Instrument the KRC with analytics to surface content gaps and product UX debt continuously
Scope
In scope
  • Nine role-aware content sections covering full platform lifecycle
  • Five user persona paths (Hiring Manager, Recruiter, HR Admin, Finance, Compliance)
  • Third-party integration documentation (Zoom, Stripe, Payoneer, e-signature)
  • Visual knowledge maps, workflow diagrams, state-transition maps
  • Search analytics instrumentation and gap-detection automation
  • Governance model: ownership, lifecycle states, update SLAs
Out of scope
  • Internal engineering documentation or API reference
  • Product feature development or UI changes
  • Customer training delivery (live webinars, 1:1 sessions)
  • Third-party platform documentation (Zoom Help Center, Stripe Docs)

Constraints & assumptions
Constraints
  • No dedicated technical writing resource; authored by COO
  • Must integrate with existing HubSpot Service Hub infrastructure
  • Content must be maintainable by non-technical team members post-launch
  • Product changes require article updates within 5 business days
Assumptions
  • CSM team will participate in monthly navigability testing
  • Engineering will provide monthly change logs for doc sync
  • Support ticket data accessible for gap-detection automation
  • Early-access customers available for beta feedback within 4 weeks of draft
Risks
  • Content drift if product ships changes without triggering doc updates — mitigated by engineering change log process and 5-day update SLA
  • Low customer adoption if KRC is not surfaced at onboarding touchpoints — mitigated by CSM hand-off protocol and in-platform link placement
  • Single point of failure on content ownership (COO-authored) — mitigated by secondary owner assignments and quarterly certification model
