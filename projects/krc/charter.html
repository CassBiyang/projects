PROJECT MANAGEMENT ARTIFACT · CHARTER Project Charter — Knowledge Resource Center Growmotely Inc.  ·  Customer-Facing Documentation System  ·  Version 1.0 PROJECT SPONSOR Sarah Hawley, CEO PROJECT OWNER Cass Biyang, COO DEPARTMENT Customer Success & Operations START DATE Q3 2021 GO-LIVE TARGET Q4 2021 STATUS    PRIORITY Completed High PROBLEM STATEMENT Growmotely's customers — mid-market hiring companies and global enterprise recruitment teams — had no structured self-serve path for onboarding or ongoing platform guidance. All onboarding relied on 1:1 CSM walkthroughs (3–4 hours per customer), support tickets clustered around repetitive procedural questions, and internal knowledge was scattered across product docs, engineering specs, and Slack threads with no role-aware, searchable single source of truth. OBJECTIVES Deflect tier-1 support volume by enabling customer self-service for procedural questions Reduce CSM onboarding time per new customer from 3–4 hours to under 1 hour Establish a single, maintained source of truth for platform behavior, integrations, and workflows Build role-aware content paths for five distinct user personas across the platform Instrument the KRC with analytics to surface content gaps and product UX debt on an ongoing basis SCOPE In scope Nine role-aware content sections covering the full platform lifecycle Five user persona paths (Hiring Manager, Recruiter, HR Admin, Finance, Compliance) Third-party integration documentation (Zoom, Stripe, Payoneer, e-signature) Visual knowledge maps, workflow diagrams, and state-transition maps Search analytics instrumentation and gap-detection automation Governance model: article ownership, lifecycle states, update SLAs Out of scope Internal engineering documentation or API reference Product feature development or UI changes Live customer training delivery (webinars, 1:1 sessions) Third-party platform documentation (Zoom Help Center, Stripe Docs) SUCCESS METRICS ≥60% Self-serve adoption rate Actual: 62% ≥25% Support ticket deflection Actual: 28% <1 hr CSM onboarding time per customer Actual: 45 min + 15 min Q&A <2 days Time to first platform use Actual: 90 min to first job posted STAKEHOLDERS STAKEHOLDER ROLE RESPONSIBILITY Sarah Hawley CEO / Sponsor Executive approval; completion feedback before broader rollout CONSTRAINTS & ASSUMPTIONS Constraints No dedicated technical writing resource; content authored by COO Must integrate with existing HubSpot Service Hub infrastructure Content must be maintainable by non-technical team members post-launch Product changes require article updates within 5 business days Assumptions CSM team will participate in monthly navigability testing sessions Engineering will provide a monthly change log for documentation sync Support ticket data will remain accessible for gap-detection automation Early-access customers are available for beta feedback within 4 weeks of initial draft RISKS R01 Content drift if product ships changes without triggering documentation updates Mitigation: Engineering change log process;
